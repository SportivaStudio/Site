Building a home workout corner that actually gets used

By Sportiva Studio · 5 min read

The gap between "I want a home gym" and actually using it every day usually isn't about equipment — it's about friction. Here's what changed once I stopped treating my setup like a storage closet and started treating it like a room with one job.

Start with the floor, not the gear

A dedicated mat or a taped-off rug boundary does more for consistency than any piece of equipment.

What worked

  • One resistance band set, visible
  • A mat that stays unrolled
  • A 10-minute setup rule

What didn't

  • Dumbbells I never touched
  • Anything needing furniture moved
  • "Just in case" gear

Skip this
